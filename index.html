I help teams put AI into real work, not demos. I call that cognitive equity: expanding what people can do, without burying them in tool noise.

I run Optimization Doc and the BE Podcast Network. I founded Transformative Principal, a long-running podcast for educational leaders, and have turned hosting over so I can put more energy into the network. I write books on school leadership and finished a doctorate in 2026.

This site is my public notes and writing. What I am focused on this month: Now. Longer bio: About. Side projects and experiments: Projects. Link-in-bio: jethrojon.es.

Working on

  • Optimization Doc — AI for real recurring work: find the workflow, make the result visible, leave a system the team can keep.
  • BE Podcast Network — production, advertising, and the show roster.
  • P-Day Emails — help missionaries write home consistently.
  • Books — SchoolX, How to Be a Transformative Principal. Doctoral work: DrJethro.com.
