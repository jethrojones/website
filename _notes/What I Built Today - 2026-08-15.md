Today I worked with AI on building and publishing [Tile Manager](https://github.com/jethrojones/tile-manager), a desktop plugin for assigning applications to named workspaces. I enabled it and tested it locally. Compatibility validation passed, and 26 model tests passed.

<!--more-->

Tile Manager adds a bar widget that lists named workspaces and a settings panel for assigning applications to them. The selected workspace is highlighted as a full row, while the settings stay at the bottom for a consistent layout. The stock numbered workspace widget can be hidden. Assigned applications can launch at login and follow me to their assigned workspace.

I submitted Tile Manager to the marketplace as [submission #302](https://github.com/HANCORE-linux/omarchy-plugin-marketplace/issues/302), where it is waiting for maintainer listing review. Marketplace review found a carriage-return escaping issue in the generated workspace rules. I fixed it and requested re-review.
