Pioneer food is the theme for our August 2026 [[Fast Sunday Family Feast]] in honor of Pioneer Day.

{% if page.image %} <img src="{{ page.image }}" alt="Cover of the Nauvoo Pioneer Cookbook, From Nauvoo to the Great Salt Lake"> {% endif %}

Staci and I went to St. Louis for my graduation this spring and took a quick jaunt up to Nauvoo. While we were there, we picked up the *Nauvoo Pioneer Cookbook: From Nauvoo to the Great Salt Lake*. Today we are making some of the pioneer recipes from the book.

Yes, our Pioneer Day feast is a couple of weeks late, but it’s the thought that counts.

## The menu

Here are the recipes from the cookbook that we are making:

- [Granny’s Buttermilk Biscuits](https://recipes.crouton.app/recipes/E9E87B24-FE21-4E87-B609-ED7F226518B8?locale=en)
- [Creamy Potatoes Au Gratin](https://recipes.crouton.app/recipes/37D2062E-1790-4F8E-8649-754D7888A61E?locale=en)
- [Brown Sugar Zucchini Bread](https://recipes.crouton.app/recipes/35ECCEF8-B519-4605-8661-E77ADB36028D?locale=en)
- [Porter Rockwell’s Roasted-Chuck Chili](https://recipes.crouton.app/recipes/52AB5A15-A5EF-4BD5-8EB6-FCA251FFB8C7?locale=en)
- [Maple Butter Roasted Chicken](https://recipes.crouton.app/recipes/7F01043C-3B12-4D72-9AB6-79064BB62FEE?locale=en)
- [Bacon, Onion, Mushroom Mashed Potatoes](https://recipes.crouton.app/recipes/F6650EC8-1C28-4B06-B2BA-86968F4DD238?locale=en)
