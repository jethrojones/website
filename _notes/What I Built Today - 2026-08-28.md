Today I worked with AI on repairing Omapresent, completing its renderer, and publishing its first public release. Omapresent is a public Markdown presentation app for Omarchy. I published [Omapresent v0.1.0](https://github.com/jethrojones/omapresent/releases/tag/v0.1.0) on August 28.

<!--more-->

Presentation and audience views now reflow after tiled-window resizing. Recall overlays show the complete slide on the first keypress, and the wide editor preview follows the slide at the caret. I added a visible Present control and launch guidance.

Remote images make no request until someone explicitly loads them. Published speaker notes can appear as subtitles and can be hidden or restored. Published title fallbacks and missing-image placeholders were completed.

The v0.1.0 release supports Markdown editing and live preview, one or two displays, fragments, notes, recall slides, mirrored scrolling, PDF/offline web export, and local and explicitly loaded remote media.

After v0.1.0, I added separate native Wayland windows for the editor and audience as a v0.1.1 checkpoint. v0.1.1 was not published.

Testing passed: 520 C++ tests, 43 renderer tests, and the release workflow. Package availability remains pending external repository review.
