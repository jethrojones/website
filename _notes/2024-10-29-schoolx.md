      <h2 id="schoolx-intro-title">Become a designer, not just a manager.</h2>
      <p>School leaders face complex problems that do not yield to another program or top-down mandate. <em>SchoolX</em> applies an iterative, human-centered design process to the daily work of leading a school.</p>
      <p>The work starts with empathy: seeing the school through the eyes of the people who experience it. From there, leaders can define the right problem, test practical ideas, learn from the results, and improve the experience again.</p>
