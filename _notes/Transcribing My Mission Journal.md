In 2000, I served a mission for [The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints](https://churchofjesuschrist.org) to Russia.

I wrote in my journal every single day.

As my [daughter](https://sisterjones.pdayemails.com) just started her mission this week, I have been transcribing my journals from messy handwriting to typed.

(Naturally, I'm recording and having AI transcribe.)

Spiritually, it's incredibly humbling reading my words from so long ago. I had faith back then, but it was so young and naive.

Today, it's the same faith, but it is stronger, more tested, and deeper.

But the really amazing thing is seeing God's hand in my life over the last 26 years. He knows me. He knows you.

Even when life is challenging, which it is right now, I know He cares about me and will help me through whatever challenges I'm facing.

For the nerds in the house, I'm using [AudioPen](https://audiopen.ai/?aff=neRMq) with a custom trigger that adds my recording to Obsidian with proper YAML frontmatter, wikilinks for names, and gives it a title. Honestly, that tedious stuff that I want to include is what has held me back all this time! I just can't be consistent with including those things and they still take such a long time! But my AI agent wrote a deterministic script to add that stuff for me automatically every time.
